## Introduction

Taking the step from research and development to market introduction and growth is the proper time to reflect on how we wish to be seen through the eyes of others.

Real Time Web has the required organizations set to grow. It is in our interest to find common ground to cooperate and evolve from, based on who we are, and where we wish to go as a company.

To address these issues we have developed a brand strategy. This strategy will help us make coherent decisions and communicate consistently over time and geographies, to maximize our resources and reach our goals.

The strategy is valid as long as it makes sense and does the job it is supposed to.

A brand strategy must be seen as a framework, which ensure direction, while still maintaining the everyday need to improvise and to be creative. We are all responsible to bring the strategy to life and invest our best efforts to make it happen.
